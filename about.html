ABOUT

WHAT IS THE FESTIVAL?

Partenope is a multidisciplinary creative festival that
brings together graphic designers, visual artists, and
innovative thinkers to explore contemporary visual
culture, share ideas, and create meaningful connections
through exhibitions, installations, talks, and collaborative
experiences.

ORIGIN

Partenope was born from a group of artists from Naples
who relocated to Amsterdam, bringing their cultural
heritage and creative vision with them. The festival
emerged as a space to connect different communities,
disciplines, and perspectives through creativity.

MISSION

Partenope’s mission is to bring together diverse creative
voices, disciplines, and ideas, creating an open platform
where emerging and established talents can share their
work, connect with others, and inspire through collective
experiences.
